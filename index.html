What's sound of childhood?

Sounds of Childhood is a website that explores Nintendo's history through its composers, telling childhood games from a sound perspective.

The project features five composers — Koji Kondo, Mahito Yokota, Kazumi Totaka, Toru Minegishi, and Kenta Nagata — and the soundtracks that have defined generations of players.

Concept

Title

Sounds of Childhood is born from the union of two passions — music and video games — and the idea of telling the games of my childhood from a sound perspective. The project explores the relationship between sound and memory, turning nostalgia into an interactive browsing experience. The monochromatic palette (white, gray, black) is inspired by the musical staff; the typography combines KG Blank Space for titles and Montserrat for body text; the images use a paper-cut effect that evokes a memory album.

Typography and Palette

The typographic choice combines KG Blank Space for the titles - with its sketchy stroke that evokes handcraft - and Montserrat for the body text. The palette is strictly monochromatic: #EFEFEF, #ACACAC, #080808, inspired by the musical staff.

FROM PROJECT TO CASE STUDY

This page is also a project

To tell the story of Sounds of Childhood, I designed a second interface: this page, built with components from GitHub's Primer Brand design system. It's not just documentation but an applied design exercise.

I started with the documentation and GitHub pages: typography, colors, spacing, and behaviors form a set of decisions. I used Primer Brand as the vocabulary to build my page.

I selected the materials and gave each content a form: Pillar for reading levels, Tabs for views, Testimonial for reflection. Hierarchies and spacing connect the chapters; on mobile, the layout changes.

The work continues in the browser: I check readability, links, and keyboard controls at different widths. The practical test also requires explaining and modifying code, keeping Figma and site consistent, and publishing the work with GitHub Pages.
