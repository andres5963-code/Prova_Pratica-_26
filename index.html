What's sound of childhood?

Sounds of Childhood is a website that explores Nintendo's history through its composers, telling childhood games from a sound perspective.

The project features five composers — Koji Kondo, Mahito Yokota, Kazumi Totaka, Toru Minegishi, and Kenta Nagata — and the soundtracks that have defined generations of players.
